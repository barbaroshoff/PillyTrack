# Privacy Policy — PillyTrack

**Last updated:** 2026-09-13

## What data we collect

PillyTrack **does not collect any personal data** and does not transmit any information to external servers.

All data entered in the app (medication names, schedules, intake history) is stored **exclusively on your device** using the local SQLite database.

## What data stays on device

| Data | Where stored | Transmitted |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| Medication names | SQLite (device) | Never |
| Intake schedules | SQLite (device) | Never |
| Intake history | SQLite (device) | Never |
| Package photos | Device file system | Never |
| Language preference | AsyncStorage (device) | Never |
| Font size preference | AsyncStorage (device) | Never |

## Camera

The camera is used **only** to scan barcodes on medication packages. No photos or videos are taken or stored during scanning. The app may optionally save a photo of the package if you choose to add one — this photo stays on your device only.

## Notifications

PillyTrack uses local notifications scheduled on your device to remind you about medication intake times. These notifications are generated entirely on-device and do not require internet access.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data stored by PillyTrack. There is no cloud backup.

## Third-party services

PillyTrack does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Medical disclaimer

PillyTrack is **not a medical application** and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. Do not change dosage or schedule without consulting a healthcare professional.
